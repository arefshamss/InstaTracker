      📥 How to Download Your Instagram Data

      1. Open Instagram and go to your profile.
      2. Tap the three-line menu (☰).
      3. Go to Accounts Center or Meta Account.
      4. Select Your information and permissions.
      5. Open Export your information.
      6. Tap Create export.
        • If you have multiple accounts, select the account you want to export.
      7. Choose Export to device.
      8. Select Customize information
        • Tap Clear all to deselect all options.
        • Select only Followers and following.
        • Tap Save.
      9. Make sure the Date Range is set to All time.
      10. Choose either format HTML or JSON as the file Format.
      11. Tap Start export.
      12. Enter your Instagram password and tap Continue.
      13. Instagram will send you an email when your export is ready.
        • This may take anywhere from a few minutes to several hours.
